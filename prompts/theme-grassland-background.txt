Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: 16:9 background plate for an interactive Chinese cartoon desktop theme named 青青草原 / 狼堡.
Primary request: the sunny, cheerful grassland world of Pleasant Goat and Big Big Wolf (喜羊羊与灰太狼), with vivid green rolling meadows, soft distant hills, a few fluffy clouds and little flowers at the edge. Clean charming Chinese 2D television animation aesthetic, simple confident outlines, large flat color shapes, restrained two-tone cel shading, warm daylight.
Camera and composition: slightly elevated oblique view of the grassland; sky and distant hills only in the upper 25 percent. The bottom 75 percent is broad continuous open meadow. The far-left 25 percent has a quiet flat grassy clearing reserved for a separately composited wolf castle. The middle and right meadow stay especially open for small sheep sprites walking around. A few small fence segments and shrubs may frame the far background and outer edges, but must not divide the open meadow into narrow paths.
Constraints: this is only the static environment layer. Do NOT draw the castle, any building, sheep, goats, wolves, characters or moving objects; these will be separate interactive layers. No water, ocean, giant river or beach. No text, logos, UI, buttons, desktop file icons, frames, collage, grid, photograph, 3D render or blurry painted textures. Beautiful readable illustration at desktop size, landscape 16:9.
